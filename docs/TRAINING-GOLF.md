# Golf Performance Workout Plan
## September 2026 → Spring 2027 Tournament Season

> The program the Train tab runs. The engine is `backend/app/services/golf_program.py`; this
> document is the source it encodes. Owner-supplied 2026-09-10.

**Schedule:** 4 core sessions + 1 optional session each week.
**Hard limit:** 70 minutes per session, including warm-up, rest and transitions.
**Priorities:** Strength and muscle, rotational power, a resilient back and core, mobile hips and shoulders, and running fitness that supports golf.
**Equipment:** Dumbbells, cables, a secured landmine, trap bar, squat equipment, pull-up bar or pulldown, kettlebell, and an approved jumping area. **No medicine-ball throws or tosses.**

## 1. Weekly schedule

| Day | Session | Target time |
|---|---|---:|
| Monday | 1 — Lower-body strength, back and core | 65–70 min |
| Tuesday | 2 — Rotational power, upper body and running | 65–70 min |
| Wednesday | Rest, golf practice or gentle mobility | — |
| Thursday | 3 — Full-body strength and trunk control | 65–70 min |
| Friday | Optional 5 — Easy cardio and mobility | 45–60 min |
| Saturday | 4 — Power and athleticism | 60–65 min |
| Sunday | Rest or golf | — |

Four-session week: Sessions 1–4, with the shortened Session 4 to keep a second run. Five-session week: add Session 5, kept easy. Time rule: start a timer; if a block runs long, omit its final accessory exercise or set, keep the warm-up and needed rest, never make up omitted work after 70 minutes.

**How the app applies it:** the week planner keeps the default days when they are free and moves sessions to the nearest free day around travel (from the calendar), keeping lower-body sessions 48 h apart. Travel days get the 8-minute mobility routine. When there aren't enough free days, lower-priority sessions are dropped, not stacked. A tournament in the week switches to the taper in §9.

## 2. Reading the workouts

3 × 8/side = three sets of eight per side. DB dumbbell, RDL Romanian deadlift, RPE effort 1–10. Strength sets finish with 2–3 good reps left (RPE 7–8; September easier). Main lifts rest 2–3 min; accessories 60–90 s. Supersets alternate two exercises. Power first, light enough to move fast; 90–120 s between power sets. Main-lift blocks include 2–4 build-up sets that don't count. Tables show the November–January reference dose; §8 adjusts by month.

## 3. Session 1 — Lower-body strength, back and core (70 min)

Warm-up 8 min: easy walk/bike/jog 2 min · 90/90 hip switches 6/side · world's greatest stretch 3/side · glute bridge 10 · bodyweight squat 8 · thoracic rotation 5/side.

| Order | Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Box jump | 3 × 3 | Modest box; land quietly, step down |
| B | Trap-bar deadlift | 4 × 4–5 | Brace; ~2 reps in reserve |
| C | Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 6–8/leg | Weaker side first |
| D1 | Chest-supported DB row | 3 × 6–8 | Superset with bench |
| D2 | DB bench press | 3 × 6–8 | Stop before grinding |
| E1 | Pallof press | 2 × 8/side | Brief pause, arms extended |
| E2 | Suitcase carry | 2 × 30–40 s/side | Walk tall |

Accessory rotation: every other week, single-leg RDL 2 × 8/leg replaces the split squat.

## 4. Session 2 — Rotational power, upper body and run (70 min)

Warm-up 7 min. Power 15 min: broad jump 3 × 3; explosive cable rotation **or** landmine rotation 3 × 4/side (alternate weekly; hips → torso → arms; decelerate before the end). Upper body 25 min: A1 pull-up/pulldown 3 × 5–8, A2 half-kneeling landmine press 3 × 6/side; B1 single-arm cable row 2 × 8/side, B2 lateral raise **or** face pull 2 × 12 (alternate weekly; omit B's last round if short). Run 18 min: 3 easy · 6 × (1 hard @ RPE 7–8 + 1 easy) · 3 easy.

## 5. Session 3 — Full-body strength (70 min)

Session 1 warm-up plus unloaded hinges. A vertical jump 3 × 3 · B front squat 4 × 4–6 (goblet alternative) · C Romanian deadlift 3 × 6–8 · D1 incline DB press 3 × 8, D2 pull-up/pulldown 3 × 6–8 · E reverse lunge 2 × 8/leg **or** back extension 2 × 8–12 (alternate weekly; first to omit) · F1 cable chop 2 × 8/side · F2 side plank 2 × 30–45 s/side.

## 6. Session 4 — Power and athleticism (65 min)

Warm-up 10 min (mobility sequence + rehearsal sets). Power 25 min, one per slot: jump (box jump A / vertical jump B) 3 × 3 · rotation (landmine rotational press A / split-stance cable rotational punch B) 3 × 4/side · third (kettlebell swing 3 × 6 A / lateral bound 3 × 3/side B). Athletic strength 20 min: walking DB lunge 2 × 8/leg · cable row 3 × 10 with push-ups 3 × 10–15 · farmer carry 2 × 40 s.

Four-session-week version (65 min): jump + rotation only (≤ 3 sets each); cable rows and push-ups 2 sets; no lunges or carries; 18 min conversational run. Do not fold Session 5's run into it.

## 7. Optional Session 5 and daily mobility

Session 5: 30–45 min conversational running (bike/elliptical/incline walk if impact interferes with golf) + 10–15 min mobility; 40–60 min total.

Daily mobility (~8 min): 90/90 hip switches 6–8/side · adductor rockbacks 6–8/side · half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch with reach 30 s/side · open-book thoracic rotations 6/side · supported deep squat hold 30 s · wall slides 8–10 · shoulder CARs 2/side. On Session 5 add thoracic extension over a roller 6–8, lat stretch 30 s/side, band external rotation 2 × 10/side.

## 8. Progression, September 2026 → spring 2027

Pre-tournament phase starts 4–6 weeks before the real first event (the app uses the earliest tournament you enter; default late April 2027). Phase rules replace the table doses; a lighter week overrides the phase.

| Period | Strength | Power | Running and golf |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 8–30 · baseline | 2 working sets per lift, RPE 6–7, main lifts 6–8; main lifts to 3 sets after two weeks if recovery is good; accessories stay at 2 | 2 sets; low jumps, light rotations | S2: 15–18 min easy run/walk · S5: 20–30 min easy |
| October · build | Main 3 × 6–8; others 2–3 sets; 2–3 reps left | 2–3 sets; faster with control | 4 × 1/1 inside 18 min; S5 toward 30–35 min |
| Nov–Dec · strength | Reference: trap bar 4 × 4–5, front squat 4 × 4–6; RPE 7–8 | usually 3 sets | toward 6 intervals; S5 30–40 min |
| January · consolidate | Reference doses; load only when earned; 3 main sets if fatigue rises; no max testing | 3 quality sets | hold intervals; S5 up to 40–45 min |
| Feb–Mar · golf power | Main 2–3 × 3–5 at RPE 7–8; accessories 2 sets; omit S3 optional and S4 lunges when needed | 3 × 3–4; swings 6 | 4–6 intervals only if fresh; S5 25–40 min; more golf |
| Final 4–6 weeks · arrive fresh | Sets down 30–40 %: 2 per main, 1–2 per accessory; familiar loads | 2–3 × 2–3; swings 2 × 5; no new drills | intervals → 15–20 min easy; S5 20–30 min |
| Apr–Jun · in season | Two short strength sessions: 2 × 3–5 main, 1–2 accessory | one short power session | four sessions of 20–50 min; optional fifth is recovery |

Lighter weeks every 4–6 weeks (start: weeks of Sep 28, Nov 2, Dec 7, Jan 11, Feb 15; then around tournaments): sets down a third to a half, RPE 6–7, power sets halved, running easy; resume from the previous load.

Double progression within the phase's rep range: pick a weight for the bottom of the range with 2–3 reps left; add reps; when every set hits the top with ~2 left, add the smallest useful increment (about 5 lb upper, 5–10 lb lower) and return to the bottom. Unilateral: the weaker side sets the load. Power: control and speed before load. Running: one interval round or ~5 easy minutes, not both, when recovery is good.

## 9. Tournament week (Saturday event; shift to match)

Mon 35–45 min strength (trap bar 2 × 3–4; DB bench and row 2 × 6; Pallof 2 × 6/side) · Tue 25–35 min easy cardio + mobility · Wed 25–35 min primer (jump 2 × 2; cable rotation 2 × 3/side; front squat 2 × 3 @ RPE 6–7; pulldown 2 × 6) · Thu 15–20 min easy walk/bike + mobility · Fri rest or brief familiar warm-up · Sat–Sun tournament; no catch-up work. Demanding lower-body work stays 48–72 h before the event.

## 10. Tracking

Baseline in September, review monthly after a lighter week; substitute checks for normal sets. Log main-lift load/reps/reps left, pull-up reps or pulldown load, unilateral stability, best of 3 controlled broad jumps once familiar, driver speed if available, easy-run duration and recovery, mobility range, readiness (sleep, soreness, energy, golf quality). Adjustment rule: two consecutive down sessions, soreness that changes the swing, or loads suddenly feeling much harder → halve accessory sets and replace intervals with easy cardio for that week.

The weekly log lives at `GET /api/v1/train/log` and on the Train tab.
